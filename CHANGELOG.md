# Changelog
## r4.0 — 2026-07-27

- Published the complete standalone r4.0 manuscript and
  its executable model outputs as one coherent release.
- Registered Markdown plus standalone HTML as the r4.0 publication format;
  the manuscript build does not generate or commit a PDF.
- Distinguished rate-limited Earth-fed orbital deployment from endogenous
  industrial reproduction and scoped the reference system to uncrewed,
  machine-only industry.
- Added an 18-sector, 17-material integrated-bootstrap model with explicit
  element bills of material, lunar and composite-asteroid yields,
  Earth strategic-material import stock, and Earth–Moon–orbit–asteroid
  transfer arcs.
- Implemented minimum-mass seed sizing, task-resolved embodied autonomy,
  residual intervention, process-specific gravity/centrifuge overhead,
  commissioning lags, yield ramps, minimum viable scale, recycling, and an
  aggregate operating-capacity doubling threshold.
- Operationalized a stylized compute–intelligence–autonomy–productivity
  loop and added terrestrial and Earth-fed orbital rate/ceiling
  counterfactuals.
- Split electronics into seven production tiers and added aggregate
  active/derelict/fragment debris dynamics with a last-five-year debris
  reproduction ratio.
- Added four central bootstrap scenarios, deterministic named uncertainty
  ensembles, 33 generated CSV tables, five figures, a report, focused
  invariants, and an independent sha256 artifact manifest.
- Recorded every coefficient as ASSUMPTION-class unless sourced and
  explicitly separated occurrence from recoverable yield, inventory from
  reserve, ensemble frequency from probability, and physical coupling from
  complete mature-ecology economics.
- Applied the release-integrity review to r4.0: repaired
  inline MathML rendering and `@35` citation corruption; removed stale LEO,
  universal-electronics, lunar-corridor, and unit-invalid retooling claims;
  elevated the abstract-seed boundary; documented the AI1 mass/capex split;
  corrected S27; softened the noisy clock sweep; and added HTML regressions.
- Initially exposed the S36 claim-level bibliography as explicit
  calibration debt (subsequently closed by the r4.0 provenance audit below)
  and made untagged release metadata honest: no mutable canonical URL,
  anticipated publication date, or concept-DOI-as-version-DOI.
- Kept the post-review analysis inside r4.0 and aligned the manuscript,
  implementation, scenario names, generated tables, and figures: a continuous
  two-cell-equivalent crossing is now aggregate operating-capacity doubling;
  five-year direction is terminal-window expansion; assumption-ensemble
  counts are design-space occupancy; and “vitamin stock” is reserved as a
  historical term rather than the name of a 27%-of-seed strategic import.
- Added r4.0 terminal-window endpoints, strategic-import-stock composition,
  zero/freeze intelligence-coupling ablations, the mass-versus-time oversize
  frontier, per-draw diagnostics, and the strict-lunar elastic infeasibility
  certificate; that derived-only pass did not change the then-current
  numerical results.
- Replaced the scalar post-seed state with all 18 sector capacities,
  preserving sector-specific commissioning lags, ramps, depreciation,
  service output, and material recovery. Aggregate cell-equivalents are now
  derived from the slowest normalized operating sector. This invalidated the
  earlier scalar crossing and inert-coupling results: the terrestrial and
  lunar bridge never double in the 80-year extension, the Moon–asteroid
  network crosses at year 34.75 and reaches 3.61 equivalents at year 40,
  and its dynamic AI coupling is operative while the
  material-starved cases remain nearly insensitive.
- Added lexicographic seed objectives for installed mass, Earth-import mass,
  and intervention demand. All three return the same central capacity vector,
  demonstrating that the current decision space contains no genuine
  multi-objective tradeoff; alternative process and redundancy variables
  remain necessary for a Pareto design study.
- Added an exhaustive dynamic primary-constraint table: the terrestrial,
  lunar-bridge, and Moon–asteroid central runs are material-limited in
  145, 146, and 48 of 160 quarterly steps; all remaining steps bind on the
  declared gross order-rate cap rather than live service capacity.
- Replaced Part III's weak “unrelated projects” baseline with a matched
  portfolio-adjacency H0 that permits shared ownership, finance, branding,
  suppliers, and ordinary commercial integration. H1 now requires observable
  cross-program technical coordination and H2 an explicit closure program;
  the evidence was re-rated and no longer claims that a coordinated
  architecture is established.
- Added independent maximin-sector, surplus-preserving, shock-aware, and
  perfect-foresight construction policies; cohort-resolved commissioning;
  architecture-specific viable floors; explicit failure/repair queues; and
  structural sweeps over shock magnitude, timing, horizon, import retention,
  surplus, and obsolescence.
- Added a delivered-seed stage with packaging, unloading/site equipment,
  calibration, spares, startup energy, deployment rates, startup failures,
  recovery, and distinct delivered/staged/installed/commissioned/operating
  states.
- Decomposed the fixed knowledge package and exposed observable autonomy,
  diagnosis, verification, engineering-cycle, yield, repair, and recovery
  gates, including communication, verification, compute, and repair regimes.
- Added explicit nominal/adverse lunar process-environment profiles that
  alter throughput, yield, power, depreciation, feasibility, and binding
  constraints.
- Added finite orbital matter accounting and a shell-resolved debris
  diagnostic for 400-km LEO, 650-km LEO, and cislunar space with four object
  classes, avoidance saturation, cross-shell transfer, removal, capacity
  loss, and conservation tests.
- Added shelf-limited tiered-electronics architectures, symmetric Earth/space
  dynamic economics, and a 36-design composition-changing robust-seed Pareto
  search. Neither declared space market becomes self-financing within 40
  years, and every tested electronics architecture retains an advanced-logic
  or parent-tool shortfall.
- Completed Part II's previously omitted communications, ground network,
  spacecraft bus, NRE, insurance, regulatory, disposal, residual-value,
  thermal, workload, and separate-financing boundaries. The favorable
  aspirational case reverses from 0.76× under the old incomplete boundary to
  1.09× Earth; generic LEO is now 5.29× Earth centrally.
- Added a governance threat/control model with physical-state traceability,
  normal/delayed/lost-link authority, verification, rollback, and explicitly
  unvalidated residual risk.
- Added a normative claim glossary and publication split: technical paper,
  companion orbital-compute economics paper, strategy/governance case study,
  and technical supplement, while retaining the monograph as the archive.
- Completed the claim-level provenance audit for every retained
  non-`ASSUMPTION` anchor and each load-bearing [S27] strategy claim. Added a
  validated machine register and deterministic public CSV; pinned SEC claims
  to exact pages/slides; added exact DOI/NTRS locators and transformations;
  and demoted reporting-derived ranges to `ASSUMPTION` wherever the source
  did not directly support both modeled endpoints.
- Added executable, machine-validated acceptance contracts for the three
  empirical gaps that remain inside r4.0: a process-feasible closure graph,
  the Stage-0 terrestrial physical rehearsal, and symmetric economic
  calibration. Their generated registers expose grades, critical part and
  parent-tool ancestry, source/fleet evidence, physical acceptance gates,
  fault injections, workload equivalence, correlated uncertainty, audited
  cash-flow events, and assumption-promotion rules. Every result field
  remains visibly pending; these contracts do not manufacture a graph,
  experiment, or empirical fit.
- Added a release verifier that parses both Markdown manifests and checks
  every recorded artifact byte-for-byte; the publication workflow now runs
  it after deterministic regeneration.
- Added candidate and immutable-publication preflight modes for clean-tree,
  version, tag, concept-versus-version DOI, release-date, canonical-URL, HTML
  metadata, and manifest gates, plus a publication runbook. No external
  release action is performed automatically.
- Made final publication preflight require an external authorization record
  tied to the exact commit, Linux CI run, archive record, date, version DOI,
  canonical URL, responsible person, and attestation. The generated template
  is deliberately non-passing and remains outside Git to avoid a
  self-referential approval commit.
- Selected target and fallback venues for all three papers from current
  publisher instructions and added a deterministic readiness table. Drafts
  now include keywords, highlights, data/code statements, and compact
  primary-source-heavy citation sets. A minimum-reference and exact numeric
  citation-resolution check prevents an empty or ornamental bibliography
  from passing; fallback expansion, final source/style review, portal-only
  rules, author declarations, and submission approval remain explicit.
- Added empirical evidence-bundle ingestion for process closure, the Stage-0
  rehearsal, and economic calibration. The evaluators fail closed on missing
  graph stages, grades, critical parts, events, fault cases, evidence hashes,
  holdouts, cash flows, or independently witnessed decisions.
- Added deterministic, complete-shape JSON templates for all three empirical
  releases. Templates carry invalid hashes and null evidence by construction,

  bundles now require non-empty source and fleet registers linked from every

- Added a privacy-preserving external paper-authorization preflight. It binds
  the chosen venue route to exact manuscript and supplement hashes, all

- Distinguished each selected target route from its unselected fallback in
