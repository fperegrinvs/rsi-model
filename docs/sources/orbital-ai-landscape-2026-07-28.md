# Public orbital-AI program landscape — dated evidence snapshot

Accessed: 2026-07-28

Comparison class: publicly documented programs proposing or demonstrating
space-based machine-learning or data-center compute, together with the
launch, spacecraft, communications, and industrial capabilities that their
named legal entities or disclosed partners can presently supply.

This is a claim snapshot, not an exhaustive market census. It preserves the
evidence boundary used by the scenario and closure-stack registries. A later
release must repeat the competitor search and create a new dated registry
version rather than silently rewriting this one.

## SpaceXAI

Primary records:

- [SpaceX Starmind AI1 product specification](https://www.spacex.com/spacexai/starmind)
- [FCC Public Notice DA 26-113](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-113A1.pdf),
  released 2026-02-04
- [SpaceX 2026 Form S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026036936/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm),
  filed 2026-05-20
- [SpaceX prospectus amendment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026040364/spaceexplorationtechnologib.htm),
  filed 2026-06-04

The AI1 page discloses a design—not commissioned capacity—with 150 kW peak
and 120 kW average compute payload, a 150 kW solar array, 70 kW/t vehicle
efficiency, a 110 m² radiator, and a 20 m by 70 m deployed envelope. The FCC
accepted for filing, but did not authorize, an application for a
non-geostationary orbital-data-center system of up to one million satellites.
The filing record describes an integrated space, connectivity, and AI
platform with extensive in-house rocket, spacecraft, satellite,
communications, software, and AI capabilities. It also records dependence on
third parties for important materials, components, semiconductors, memory,
and compute hardware.

The Terafab framework with Tesla is prospective. Specific projects,
milestones, schedules, and capital commitments require later agreements.
Chip design, lithography operation, fabrication, memory, packaging, and test
are announced scope; no Terafab wafer or packaged-device output is disclosed.
Tesla is a separate public company and is available to a modeled joint case
only through an explicitly labelled affiliate hybrid.

## Google and Planet — Project Suncatcher

Primary records:

- [Google Project Suncatcher announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/google-project-suncatcher/),
  dated 2025-11-04
- [Planet mission announcement](https://www.planet.com/pulse/planet-to-build-and-operate-advanced-space-platform-for-google-s-project-suncatcher-moonshot/),
  dated 2025-11-04
- Google technical study, arXiv:2511.19468v2, dated 2026-06-17

Google describes a research moonshot for interconnected solar-powered
satellites using TPU accelerators. Google and Planet state that Planet will
build and operate two prototype satellites targeted for launch by early 2027
to test TPU hardware, tandem flight, and high-bandwidth crosslinks in orbit.
The technical study supplies radiation tests and a proposed 81-satellite
cluster, but explicitly leaves major thermal, communications, reliability,
repair, and full-economic boundaries unresolved. The prototypes were future
missions as of this snapshot.

## Starcloud

Primary record:

- [Starcloud-1 mission page](https://www.starcloud.com/starcloud-1), accessed
  2026-07-28

Starcloud states that Starcloud-1 launched in November 2025 carrying an
NVIDIA H100, ran a version of Gemini in space, and trained a nanoGPT model in
December. These are company statements about an orbital demonstrator. They
do not establish an operating data-center constellation, internally produced
accelerators, launch capacity, or reproductive industrial closure.

## Blue Origin — Project Sunrise

Primary record:

- [FCC Applications Accepted for Filing, Report SAT-01992](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-420864A1.pdf),
  released 2026-04-17, pp. 1 and 4

The FCC notice says that Blue Origin requested authority to deploy and
operate up to 51,600 Project Sunrise satellites as a space data center in
500–1,800 km circular sun-synchronous orbits. The application was accepted
for filing after initial review; the notice expressly reserves further
review and possible return. This is an application, not deployment
authorization or an operating constellation.

## Actor-boundary cross-check

Primary record:

- [Tesla 2026 Q1 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026026673/tsla-20260331.htm),
  filed 2026-04-23, pp. 17 and 27–31

Tesla says that it has significant influence over, but does not control, its
SpaceX equity investment. It reports ordinary-course SpaceX purchases of
Megapack products in 2026 Q1 and describes Tesla's separate energy,
manufacturing, AI, battery-material, semiconductor, and Optimus programs.
Those disclosures establish a related-party transaction and possible
portfolio adjacency. They do not make Tesla assets part of SpaceXAI or
guarantee transfer, space qualification, availability, or joint control.

## Negative-evidence protocol

The search covered the primary records above, the registered independent
studies [S39–S44], current company program pages, and named regulatory
notices. Job advertisements, generic inferences from company type, and
third-party rumors were not accepted as evidence of operating capability.
`no_evidence` means that no qualifying public record was found within this
boundary; it is not proof that a capability is absent.
